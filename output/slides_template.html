Limits and next steps

From prototype to a real studio

Test the kitchen

Bake every sourced recipe and time each task, so the capacity model uses real hours.

Real payments

Hosted checkout (Stripe or Square). I skipped it on purpose to avoid cost and any risk of leaking financial data.

Healthy margins

Price the shopping list against local grocery stores and Costco, then buy in bulk from a wholesale supplier.

Set up the business

CT cottage-food licence (sales cap $50k; check which fillings are allowed), LLC, insurance, and paying myself a wage.

Google sync

OAuth on a studio account for two-way calendar sync and sending approved emails.

My recipes, online

Replace the sample recipes with my own, then host the app for real customers.
